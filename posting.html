How a clip reaches TikTok

Posting happens inside the Windows app. This page is the public description of that flow. There is nothing to sign in to on this website.

1. Connect the creator’s account

In Settings, the creator presses Connect next to TikTok. The browser opens TikTok’s login and permission screen. Barticus Studio never asks for the TikTok password.

The app requests four permissions:

  • user.info.basic — read the display name and show which account is connected.
  • video.list — list that account’s recent video titles in Settings.
  • video.upload — upload the clip file the creator selected.
  • video.publish — publish that clip with the settings the creator chose on the post screen.

2. Review the clip

The app shows the finished clip. The creator can change the caption or discard the clip. Nothing is sent to TikTok at this step.

3. Choose the post settings

Before the upload, the post screen shows:

  • the connected TikTok display name
  • who can watch: everyone, friends, followers, or only the creator
  • switches for comments, Duet, and Stitch, including any option TikTok has disabled for that account
  • a checkbox for TikTok’s music confirmation

Post stays off until the creator picks who can watch and ticks the music confirmation.

4. Upload

Post sends that one video, the caption, and the chosen settings to TikTok through TikTok’s Content Posting API. The app then shows whether the post went out.

Disconnect

Disconnect in the app deletes the saved TikTok connection on that PC. The creator can also remove Barticus Studio in TikTok under Settings, Security, and Apps and websites.
