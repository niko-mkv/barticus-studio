Barticus Studio

Barticus Studio is a Windows desktop app. It cuts short World of Warcraft clips on the creator’s PC and can post a finished clip to that creator’s own TikTok account.

This website explains the product. It has no login, no account, and no download gate. The app itself runs on Windows.

What the app does

  • Watches a World of Warcraft session for boss fights, wipes, deaths, and rare enemies.
  • Cuts a short clip from the local recording and shows it in the app.
  • Lets the creator edit the caption, throw the clip away, or post it.
  • Posts only after the creator connects their own TikTok account and confirms the post.

Who it is for

One creator uses Barticus Studio on their own computer for their own channel. The app does not post to other people’s accounts, and it does not browse TikTok for them.

TikTok connection

The creator presses Connect in the app. TikTok’s own login page asks them to allow Barticus Studio. After that, the app shows the TikTok display name and the creator’s recent videos.

On the post screen the creator chooses who can watch, and whether comments, Duet, and Stitch are on. They confirm TikTok’s music terms, then press Post. The app uploads that one file.

The full flow is on the TikTok posting page.

What stays on the PC

Clips, captions, and the TikTok connection stay on the creator’s computer until they post. A post sends TikTok the video, the caption, and the settings the creator just confirmed. Barticus Studio does not sell that data.
