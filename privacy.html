Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. This policy covers the Barticus Studio website and the Barticus Studio Windows app, including its TikTok connection.

Who we are

Privacy requests for Barticus Studio go to niko.mkv@gmail.com or barticus.stierbaer@gmail.com.

This website

The site is static. It has no account, no login form, no newsletter, and no cookies set by Barticus Studio. It does not collect names, emails, or TikTok data.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. That processing is GitHub’s, not a form on this site.

The desktop app

Barticus Studio runs on the creator’s Windows PC. Gameplay clips, captions, and app settings are stored on that PC. They are not uploaded to a Barticus Studio server. There is no Barticus Studio account.

TikTok data

If the creator connects TikTok, the app receives what those permissions return:

  • display name, from user.info.basic, shown on the account card and the post screen
  • recent video titles for that account, from video.list, shown in Settings
  • an access token and a refresh token, so the creator does not have to log in before every post
  • the video file, caption, and post settings the creator confirms, sent to TikTok with video.upload and video.publish

The app does not read TikTok messages, comments, followers, or analytics. It does not ask for the TikTok password. It does not post a clip unless the creator confirms that post.

Why this data is used

The TikTok data is used only to show the connected account and to publish a clip the creator chose. The legal basis is the creator’s request to connect the account and to post. Tokens stay on the PC while the connection is active.

Sharing

A confirmed post is sent to TikTok. Barticus Studio does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertisers or other apps.

How long it is kept

Disconnect in the app deletes the TikTok tokens stored on that PC. Removing Barticus Studio in TikTok’s settings stops further access. Clips stay on the PC until the creator deletes them.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, or deletion of personal data Barticus Studio holds, and you can withdraw the TikTok permission at any time. Email niko.mkv@gmail.com or barticus.stierbaer@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority, dsb.gv.at.
