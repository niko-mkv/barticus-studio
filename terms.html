Terms of Service

Last updated: 18 November 2026. These terms cover the Barticus Studio website and the Barticus Studio Windows app.

The product

Barticus Studio is desktop software for one creator. It cuts short gameplay clips on that creator’s PC and can send a clip to the creator’s own TikTok account. The website explains the product. It does not host accounts or a login.

Your content

You keep the clips you record. You post only footage you have the right to post. You choose the caption, who can watch, and the comment, Duet, and Stitch settings before anything is sent.

TikTok

TikTok posting uses TikTok’s login and Content Posting API. TikTok’s own terms apply to the account and to the post. Barticus Studio does not control who TikTok shows the video to after you publish it. You can disconnect the app at any time.

Acceptable use

Do not use the app to post someone else’s account, to hide the source of a video, or to break TikTok’s rules or the law. The app is not a way around a platform ban.

Price

There is no subscription and no payment inside the app or on this website.

Availability

The app is provided as it is. A game update, a recording setup, or a TikTok API change can stop a feature. There is no promise that every clip will post, or that TikTok will keep the post public.
